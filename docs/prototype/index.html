A little more about your shortlist

Suggestions use recorded occasion tags, neighborhood, and an optional all-in budget estimate. Both “fun” and “good for talking” must match when you ask for both. Unknown prices are excluded from budget searches. Imported places appear automatically, including uncategorized pins. Suggested tags from notes and list keywords are labeled as inferred. Saved-list suggestions and personal endorsements are shown separately. You can edit statuses or hide any place anytime. Visit status, personal notes, and neighborhood are optional. Visit status is shown on each card; unknown details stay unknown.

Browsing uses keyword rules. The plan conversation uses AI when configured and may make mistakes. AI reasons use your notes, recorded details, and labeled review hints. Confirm important requirements with the venue. It cannot verify dietary needs, accessibility, current prices, opening hours, or booking availability. When available, Google details and a small review sample add context to your saved places.

Where do I find the link?

In Google Maps, open You or Saved, open a list, then choose Share and Copy link. The link must allow viewing while signed out. Anyone with that link may be able to view the list.

Imports include every category. Visits and endorsements stay yours to set. Paste the same link again to add newly saved places. Matching Maps links are skipped.

Automatic access to private saves remains planned. Shared-list imports depend on Google’s page format and may occasionally be unavailable.

Bring your saved lists

Import a Google Takeout CSV. Your places are ready to explore, with statuses you can edit anytime.

How to export your list

Open Google Takeout and select Saved. Download and unzip the export, then choose a saved-list CSV here. We support title/name, URL, and note columns, including item_content_url. Other formats need review; ZIP uploads are not supported.

Preview your import

These places will be available in suggestions immediately after import, without implying a visit or personal endorsement. Keywords in notes and list names can suggest editable tags. Missing categories and prices stay unknown. Existing duplicates are skipped; no places are overwritten.
