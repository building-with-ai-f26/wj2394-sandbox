Privacy

Local prototype · Updated November 10, 2026

Your imported places, notes, statuses, and personal endorsements stay in this browser's local storage. Backups you export are files on your device. Clearing site data removes local records. This prototype has no accounts, cloud guide database, or analytics.

Google enrichment is optional and runs through a server on your computer. When enabled, matching sends a place name and optional neighborhood to Google Places. Detail and photo requests send Google place identifiers. Personal notes, visit status, and endorsements are not sent to Google. Google responses are displayed during the current session; reviews, category details, and photo resources are not written into your guide storage or backups. Only the linked Google place ID is saved so you can refresh details later.

Sending a plan sends your conversation and a compact summary of the active guide, including place names, notes, categories, neighborhoods, budgets, visit status, tags and review-derived hints, through the local server to OpenRouter and its model provider. Available Google descriptions, price ranges and up to five review texts per candidate are included for ranking. Photos are excluded. Conversation history stays in page memory until reset or reload. The private local usage ledger records request metadata, provider-reported tokens and cost without message or place contents.

Photos and categories may be unavailable, and a review sample is not a complete picture of a place. Google processes API requests under its own policies. Visit the Google Privacy Policy and Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service. Google Maps links and attribution links take you to external services. GitHub hosts the static public pages and may process requests under its own policies.

The developer-facing API key remains on your local server. This prototype is for local evaluation and does not offer a public Google enrichment service. To report a privacy issue, open a repository issue without posting private data or credentials.
